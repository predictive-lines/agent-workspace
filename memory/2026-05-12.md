## Payroll clarification — Michigan UIA
- Justin asked whether making unemployment insurance payments to the state is part of payroll.
- Answer given: yes, state unemployment insurance is best treated as part of payroll compliance, even though it is employer-paid and not withheld from employee paychecks. Map: employee withholding = fed/state income + employee FICA; employer payroll taxes = employer FICA + FUTA + state UIA; payroll-adjacent filings/payments = quarterly wage/UI reports, workers comp payroll audits, union fringe reporting.

## Payroll provider responsibility clarification
- Justin asked whether employee withholdings and remittances to relevant authorities are handled by payroll providers like QuickBooks Payroll.

## Office audio AI pipeline — Ubiquiti microphone expansion options
- Justin asked about using two Ubiquiti cameras to cover office room edges plus one USB mic on a center PC, but was hesitant to trust integrated security-camera microphones. He asked for actual microphone expansion options.
- Research summary given: normal UniFi Protect cameras generally do not have practical external mic expansion. Models either have integrated mic or no audio; e.g., G5 Pro tech specs list `Audio: Microphone`, not 3.5mm/XLR/USB/line-in.
- Real UniFi exception: AI Theta ecosystem. AI Theta Hub has a dedicated `Theta audio port`; Ubiquiti sells `AI Theta Audio`, a two-way wall/flush audio module with microphone+speaker that connects to the AI Theta Hub using proprietary Theta connector. AI Theta Audio tech specs show USB-C power to the module but cable connector is proprietary Theta; Hub has `Theta audio port` and `Theta lens port`. There is a 1m audio cable and a 10m extension cable.
- Potential edge case: third-party ONVIF camera with audio via UniFi AI Port, but this is not external mic expansion for a UniFi camera and should not be trusted without testing.
- Recommendation: cameras should be visual context / room coverage only; use a PC-connected USB conference mic as source of truth for transcription. If Ubiquiti-native remote audio is needed, use AI Theta + AI Theta Audio, but note proprietary ecosystem/cabling.

## I-9 expiration / reverification guidance for EFP personnel audit
- Justin asked whether I-9s expire and whether expiration is tied to evidence provided (e.g., SS card or birth certificate lasts forever).
- Guidance given: the I-9 itself does not expire for an active employee. What can expire is employment authorization/documentation, and only some categories require reverification.
- For normal citizen/permanent-work-authorized employees: birth certificate + driver's license, unrestricted Social Security card + photo ID, U.S. passport/passport card, or similar documents do not create routine reverification just because an identity document later expires. USCIS says reverification is never required for U.S. citizens/noncitizen nationals and generally not required when U.S. passports/passport cards/green cards/List B identity docs expire.
- Reverify only for expiring employment authorization (e.g., some EAD/work permits/temporary authorization), using Supplement B attached to original I-9.
- Practical EFP conclusion: newer-hire I-9s for Connor/Konner, Chrissy/Krissy, and Josh likely do not need refresh if based on regular citizen-style documents. Remaining issue is missing/not-located I-9s for Keith/Bud, not old-but-valid I-9s for newer hires.
