### Key Design Decisions & Bugs Fixed
1. **Debt sweep waterfall:** SN2 → SN1 → SBA priority (Justin's request)
2. **$500K working capital reserve:** Sweep only fires when ending cash before sweep exceeds $500K
3. **Bug: Principal double-counting interest** — formula was `-(Payment + Interest)` instead of `-(Payment - Interest)`. SBA showed $29K/mo principal instead of correct $9.4K. Fixed to `SBA principal = -(DT!B46 - interest)`.
4. **Bug: Tax computed monthly** — was taxing profitable months at 27% without crediting loss months. Restructured: tax is annual only (computed on annual pretax total), applied to annual net change in cash. Monthly cash flow is pre-tax. Avoids circular dependency.
5. **Circular dependency chain identified:** Interest → Pretax → Tax → NI → CFO → Sweep → Balance → Interest. Broken by keeping monthly tax = 0 and computing annual tax separately.
