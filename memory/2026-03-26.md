## Profit Sharing Plan — Excel Fire Protection

### What Happened
Justin asked for a profit sharing plan policy document to help attract talent to Marquette/UP despite harsh winters. Key design decisions:

- **Hybrid two-component model:**
  - Project Profit Share: 10% of project-level gross profit, paid within 30 days of retainage collection
  - Annual Service Pool: 5% of company-level net profit, paid within 90 days of FYE
- **Allocation weights:** Foreman 1.75×, JM 1.00×, Apprentice 0.25×→0.85× (scaled by tenure with Excel Fire, not union seniority)
- **Project share also weighted by hours worked** on that project
- **Guardrails:** Min 20% gross margin / $5K GP per project, min 5% net margin for annual pool, 15% combined cap, warranty clawback
- **Service/repair pricing:** 10% internal markup to fund annual pool
- **Retainage-linked payout:** aligns cash flow (never pay before you collect) and creates direct behavioral incentive
