### S-Corp Stock vs Asset Tax Insight
- For an S-Corp, the tax difference between stock deal and asset deal is much smaller than intuition suggests
- No double-taxation issue (unlike C-Corp where asset sale = corporate tax + individual cap gains)
- The difference is only on the non-goodwill portion: ~15% of net proceeds × (37% - 23.8%) = ~$58K
- Main advantage of stock deal for S-Corp seller = covenant not to compete gets folded into cap gains

### Lesson: Inline formulas vs helper rows
- Used a helper row for the SN2 PMT formula initially; when we deleted the tax section, the helper
  row went with it, breaking year formulas. Better to inline or keep the helper in a stable location
  far from sections likely to be deleted.
