## S-Corp vs C-Corp / QSBS Tax Structure Discussion (12:30 PM)

Justin explored the tax structure decision for Excel Fire post-acquisition. Key conclusions:

**Decision direction: C-Corp from day one for QSBS eligibility.**

- S-Corp → C-Corp conversion later doesn't work cleanly for QSBS because shares held during S-Corp period aren't "originally issued" by a C-Corp
- QSBS requires shares acquired at original issuance from a C-Corp, §1202(c)(1)
- Free cash flow consumed by debt service for first 5 years anyway, making S-Corp pass-through distributions less valuable

**Deal restructure — seller retains 10% equity:**
- Kevin Masich retains 10% equity post-close with $700K buyout over time
- Seller's retained shares do NOT qualify as QSBS (not original issuance)
- Justin/Jaclyn's 90% DOES qualify as QSBS

**Corporate redemption vs cross-purchase:**
- §1202(c)(3) anti-abuse rule: corporate redemptions within 2 years before/after stock issuance exceeding 5% of total value can taint QSBS
- Cross-purchase (personal funds) avoids this but creates double-tax problem
- Key insight: redemptions AFTER the 2-year window (May 2028) are outside the testing period

**Final proposed structure:**
- C-Corp from close (May 2026)
- Kevin employed at **$133K/year salary for 3 years** (market-rate comp for licensed JM/foreman running operations during transition)
- **$301K corporate redemption of his 10% equity in Year 4+** (outside §1202(c)(3) window)
- Total = $700K to seller
- $301K aligns with ~10% of enterprise value at purchase
- After redemption + share retirement, Justin/Jaclyn hold 100% QSBS-qualified equity
- Salary is deductible operating expense; redemption is non-deductible but smaller
- Employment agreement and redemption agreement must be **separate, independent documents** — not cross-referenced

**Key legal requirements:**
- S-Corp revocation: Letter of Revocation + Statement of Consent (>50% members) mailed to IRS, effective at close date
- Must be received by IRS on or before effective date
- 5-year waiting period before re-electing S-Corp (one-way door)
- Short tax years: S-Corp Oct 1 - May 14, C-Corp May 15 - Sep 30

**QSBS details discussed:**
- $10M exclusion per taxpayer (potentially $20M between Justin & Jaclyn)
- 5-year minimum hold from issuance date
- <$50M gross assets requirement (easily met)
- Shares must be formally issued with stock certificates, board resolution, stock ledger

**Keith's salary equivalent (for comp benchmarking):**
- Current CBA: $45.24/hr × 2,080 = $94,099/year
- Apr 2026 CBA: $46.94/hr × 2,080 = $97,635/year  
- Apr 2027 CBA: $49.64/hr × 2,080 = $103,251/year
- Kevin's $133K is a reasonable premium over rank-and-file JM for license holder + transition role
