## EFP HR regional manager modeling decisions
- Justin chose not to split HR into separate People and Assignments tabs. Human Resources should be the definitive time-phased employee/officer table, with one row per person-assignment period; person attributes that change over time (role, site, home address/lat-long, pay basis, etc.) live on the row for the applicable period.
- Regional manager identity should be modeled separately from Role to avoid breaking role-keyed logic. Regional manager stipend must stay separate from hourly labor/CBA wage logic and should be treated as an OpEx driver/budget item, not conflated with the base hourly wage used for union-related calculations.
- Final compensation schema preference: use `Pay Basis` plus collapsed numeric fields `Rate`, `Annual Quantity`, and `Annual Rate` / annual amount instead of separate hourly rate, salary amount, hours/month, stipend/month columns. `Annual Quantity` was preferred over monthly quantity.
- HR should include fields: Person Name, Role, Earnings Code/Assignment Type/Pay Basis, Start Date, End Date, Active, Site, home address fields, Latitude/Longitude, QB/Account Name, Account Name / chart-of-accounts mapping, Cost Class / Include in Model, Rate, Annual Quantity, Annual Amount, Notes.
- Add/keep a KPI person-cost summary to regain a single-row-per-person, per-calendar-year cost view despite HR becoming granular by assignment period.
- Justin flagged CBA/legal caution: subsistence likely depends on the worker's home address distance; preserve home lat/long unless CBA review proves otherwise.
