## ROBS + QSBS Thread (#open-claw)

Justin asked about ROBS impact on QSBS (§1202) eligibility at sale.

Key takeaways:
- Shares held by the 401(k) plan (ROBS) **cannot** benefit from §1202 exclusion — plan is not a "noncorporate taxpayer" and gains come back as ordinary income on distribution, not capital gains
- Only personally-held shares could qualify for QSBS treatment
- If using ROBS, structure it to hold minority stake; personally hold the rest to maximize §1202 eligibility
- Also flagged: Excel Fire is structured as LLC with S-Corp election — QSBS requires C-Corp at time of share issuance. S-Corp election disqualifies future shares. Worth clarifying with tax advisor.
- Excel Fire at $3M acquisition likely clears the $50M gross assets threshold ✅
- Minimum 5-year hold required for §1202
